# Assumptions

- **Data:** Synthea synthetic data, seed 2026, 5,000 living patients, Massachusetts demographics.
- **Synthea version:** jar downloaded on ____ (kept in tools/ to keep results reproducible).
- **Bed capacity:** ____ beds per hospital (Synthea has no bed counts).
- **Readmission rule:** inpatient admission within 30 days of a prior discharge;
  same-day re-admission treated as a transfer.
- **Length of stay:** same-day stays counted as 1 day.
