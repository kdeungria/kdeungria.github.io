# Metric Definitions

| Metric | Formula | Value area |
|---|---|---|
| 30-day readmission rate | Stays followed by an inpatient admission within 30 days of discharge / eligible discharges (excl. in-hospital deaths) | Patient care + cost |
| In-hospital mortality rate | Deaths during the stay / stays | Patient care |
| 30-day mortality rate | Deaths within 30 days of discharge / discharges | Patient care |
| ALOS | Total bed-days / stays | Efficiency |
| Bed occupancy % | Occupied bed-days / (assumed beds x days) | Efficiency |
| Cost per stay | Total encounter cost / stays | Financial (supporting) |
